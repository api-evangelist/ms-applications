Microsoft 365 Copilot is brilliant at working with language. But work isn’t only words, sometimes you don’t want to describe what you need in a back-and-forth chat – you want to see it, touch it, and act on it right where you already are . Picture approving an expense, checking your remaining leave, or booking a desk – without ever leaving the chat to hunt for the right tool.
